# Phase 3 — Judgment: 3 directions scored against the Strategy-Lock rubric

All three pass the **DQ gates** (primary action = call/WhatsApp/form; minimal form; Call+WhatsApp everywhere + sticky bar; no false promises; no horizontal scroll at 390px — verified by screenshot). So all three advance to scoring.

|---|---|---|---|
| Trust proven not claimed (25%) | 5 | 5 | 4 |
| Conversion clarity / hierarchy (20%) | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| Calm warm competent register (15%) | 5 | 4 | 5 |

**Ranking: B (4.75) > A (4.50) > C (4.15)**

## Why B wins
- Scores highest on the two heaviest criteria, which *are* the one job: **trust-proven (25%)** and **conversion clarity (20%)**. Form held as a true co-star (sticky on desktop, chips + phone + form at the top on mobile) + a conversational-FAQ that defuses the 5 fears one-by-one with verbatim copy = the most direct path to "anxious family taps the button."
- **Best mobile readability** — the primary viewport.
- **No dependency on assets we don't have yet.** A and C lean on caregiver/founder **photo wells that are currently placeholders**; their full impact is conditional on real photography. B ships at full strength with *today's* content.
- Cleanest token/brand fidelity → easiest, lowest-risk port into the existing Next.js + Tailwind v4 tokens.

## What A and C do better (graft candidates)
- **A** has the most warmth + the strongest anti-slop identity (newspaper masthead, "accountability panel" rendering the open price at display scale). Its editorial price panel is a strong graft into B. When real caregiver/founder photos exist, A's photographic treatment becomes materially stronger — worth revisiting then.
- **C** is the most emotionally soothing ("reads like an exhale"), best emotional fit for anxiety. Its calm teal atmosphere is a candidate for a warmer/deeper hero option in B. Caveat: a dark atmospheric hero is the riskiest for a 60-yr-old on a low-end Android in bright sunlight (screen glare) and is the boldest departure from the "calm clean medical" brand.

## Recommendation
Build **Direction B (modern-minimal)** as the base, optionally grafting **A's display-scale open-price "accountability" panel** for extra trust weight. Revisit A's photographic editorial treatment once real caregiver/founder photos are available.
